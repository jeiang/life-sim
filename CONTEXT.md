# Life Sim

A text-first life simulation game, played offline on a phone, whose rules and content come from authored data rather than hard-coded logic.

## Language

### Building blocks

**Core**:
The content-agnostic part of the game that reads packs, advances a world, resolves probabilities, and renders generic screens. It knows life-sim primitives (age, money, loans, people, occupations, assets) but no specific content.
_Avoid_: Engine framework, runtime

**Pack**:
A bundle of authored content (storylets, stats, occupations, item kinds, people data) compiled into the game at build time.
_Avoid_: Mod, plugin, DLC

**Core loop**:
The minimum playable cycle of one life: birth, aging up year by year, school, a job, money, shopping, and random events, until death.
_Avoid_: MVP, base game

### State

**World**:
The whole game state: every person, their relationships and holdings, and a pointer to the player.
_Avoid_: Game, save, life

**Person**:
Any human in the world, including the player. Either generated by the Core from Pack data or authored by a Pack.
_Avoid_: NPC, character

**Player**:
The person the user currently controls; a pointer into the world, which can move (for example to a child).
_Avoid_: Self, protagonist, hero

**Stat**:
A Pack-declared integer from 0 to 100 on a person, shown to the user as a bar (for example Happiness, Health).
_Avoid_: Attribute, bar

**Quality**:
A Pack-declared hidden integer or flag on a person (for example has a driving licence, times arrested).
_Avoid_: Variable, state flag

**Relationship**:
A directed link from one person to another with a role (parent, sibling, partner, friend, classmate, coworker) and a closeness from 0 to 100.
_Avoid_: Connection, bond

**Asset**:
An owned instance of a Pack-defined item kind, with a purchase price, a current value, and its own qualities.
_Avoid_: Item (for the owned thing), possession, inventory

**Item kind**:
A Pack-defined template from which assets are created, such as a car model or a house type.
_Avoid_: Product, SKU

**Life**:
The span of one person's existence from birth to death, as played or simulated.
_Avoid_: Run, playthrough

**Occupation**:
A role a person holds, such as student at a school or employee in a job, with a ladder, yearly pay or tuition, and performance. A person can hold several at once, limited by the exclusivity groups their kinds declare (for example one school, one full-time job). Past occupations remain on record as history.
_Avoid_: Job, enrollment, career (as a model term)

**Money**:
An integer amount in minor units of the single currency the Pack sets.
_Avoid_: Cash balance, funds

**Loan**:
A debt with principal, yearly rate, and term, repaid automatically at each age-up.
_Avoid_: Debt, credit, negative cash

### Time and content

**Age-up**:
The single step that advances the world by exactly one year.
_Avoid_: Turn, tick, next year

**Settlement**:
The fixed phase of an age-up, after aging and before events, in which occupations pay or charge, loans take payments, and assets change value.
_Avoid_: Payday, upkeep

**Storylet**:
The single unit of authored content: conditions, choices, weighted outcomes, and effects. It is either offered in a menu (an action) or drawn at age-up (an event).
_Avoid_: Card, scenario, event (as the model term)

**Action**:
A storylet offered to the player in a menu.

**Event**:
A storylet drawn by the Core during an age-up.

**Effect**:
One change a storylet outcome makes to the world, from a fixed set the Core defines; Packs cannot invent new kinds.
_Avoid_: Command, script, side effect

**Journal**:
The per-year text record of what happened in a life, shown to the user as the feed.
_Avoid_: Log, history, timeline

**Death**:
The end of a life, caused only by a storylet's death effect, which records a cause.
_Avoid_: Game over

### Persistence

**Graveyard**:
The archive of finished lives, kept as obituaries only.
_Avoid_: Cemetery, history, finished saves

**Choice log**:
The ordered record of the seed and every player choice in a life, which replays that life on the build that made it.
_Avoid_: Replay, event log, journal
