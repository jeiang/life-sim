# Family is stored as parent links; kinship is derived

Every person, the player included, stores their parents (`Person.parents`: a list of `{ id, kind }`, `kind` being `birth`, `adopted` or `step`). Nothing else about the family is stored. What one person is to another (grandparent, half-sibling, cousin, in-law) is computed from those links by the Core (`kinshipOf(world, viewer, other)`), relative to a viewer: the player pointer in expressions and text. We rejected hand-set family roles as the source of names because they cannot say who a grandparent is, break when the player pointer moves to an heir, and need a relabel table at succession. Relationship role rows stay for closeness and for non-family roles (friend, partner, coworker); they no longer name family.

## Consequences

- Kinship ids are Core-owned and gender-neutral: `parent`, `grandparent`, `great-grandparent`, `child`, `grandchild`, `great-grandchild`, `sibling`, `half-sibling`, `step-sibling`, `adopted-sibling`, `aunt-uncle`, `great-aunt-uncle`, `cousin`, `niece-nephew`, `step-parent`, `step-child`, `spouse`, `parent-in-law`, `sibling-in-law`, `child-in-law`. Packs reference them as bare ids (no `/`). A new id is a Core release (ADR 0002). Labels (`mother`, `uncle`, ...) are Core text, chosen by the person's gender when it is male or female, neutral otherwise.
- Derivation: ancestors by birth or adoption up to three generations; the closest common ancestor (fewest generations in total, then nearest the viewer, then lowest id) gives the relation by its distances (`0,n` descendant, `n,0` ancestor, `1,1` sibling, `2,1`/`3,1` aunt or great-aunt, `1,2` niece or nephew, `2,2` cousin). Anything farther (great-nieces, second cousins, great-great-grandparents) has no kinship id. Sibling kind: they share a parent by birth or adoption; `adopted-sibling` if any shared link is adoption, else `sibling` when both have the same parents, else `half-sibling`. Step links are followed one hop only: `step-parent`, `step-child`, and `step-sibling` (two people who share a parent through a step link). A step-parent's relatives are not kin.
- In-laws come last: `spouse` (both ends of a `spouse` role row), `parent-in-law` and `sibling-in-law` through the spouse, `sibling-in-law` also for a sibling's spouse, `child-in-law` for a child's spouse.
- Succession needs no relabel map: moving `playerId` to the heir re-derives every name from the heir's position.
- Role writes keep the tree in step: `spawn_person(role, ...)` and `relationship(p).role = role` with a role whose last id segment is `parent`, `sibling`, `child` or `grandparent` add the matching parent link (a sibling takes the player's parents; a grandparent becomes a parent of the player's first parent; with no parent to attach to, nothing is added). New lives link the generated parents and siblings the same way, so they add no people.
- Save schema 6 adds `parents`. Migration from schema 5 builds the links from the player's role rows by the same rule. Adoption and step links have no effect that creates them yet; the Children and Dating issues add them through `addParentLink`.
- Expressions: `kin(person)`, `is_kin(person, id)`, `count_kin(id, min_age, max_age)` (living people), `person.kin` / `person.kin_label`. `target` accepts kinship ids as well as role ids; a bare id that is a kinship id always means the kinship.
